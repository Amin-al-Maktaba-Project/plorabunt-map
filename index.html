About this database

What this is

Plorabunt is a curated record of attacks on places of worship, on worshippers, and on religious personnel worldwide. Each entry documents a single incident and is tied to the source material from which it was reconstructed.

How to read the map

Points are aggregated by country, not by exact location. The size of a point reflects the number of recorded incidents in that country, or the number of victims killed when that metric is selected; colour deepens with the number of victims killed. A large point therefore means "many entries in this record", which is not the same as "the most violence".

Coverage and limits

The density of this record reflects the availability and the language of reporting as much as it reflects events. Countries covered intensively by English-language and international media are documented more thoroughly here than countries where reporting is sparse, restricted, or published in languages not systematically surveyed. Absence of points is evidence about the record, not about the world.

Casualty figures are given as reported. Where sources disagree, the range is preserved rather than resolved, and entries marked "Unknown" or "(Uncertain)" record a genuine gap or dispute in the underlying reporting. Attribution to a group is given only where a source states it; it is not inferred.

Inclusion criteria

An event enters this record when the victims were attacked inside the place of worship in question, or while performing a religious act in the course of a religious ceremony. The test is therefore the situation of the victims at the moment of the attack. An attack that meets one of these two conditions is recorded whether or not the motive attributed to the perpetrators is religious, disputed, or unknown.

⚠️ Still to be stated by the editors: the geographical and chronological scope deliberately surveyed, the categories of event deliberately excluded, and the procedure by which an entry is verified before publication.

Data and code

Download the published record as a spreadsheet. The file is generated from what the map currently holds, so it is always in step with what you see.

The dataset and the code that renders it are available at github.com/Amin-Al-Maktaba/plorabunt-map, where the full revision history of every record can be consulted.

Corrections

Corrections and additions are welcome. Use the Submit a case button, which opens a pre-filled report; every submission is reviewed against its source before it enters the published record, and the review remains publicly visible.
